Chef's Pro Tips

Essential Home Baker's Knowledge

Quick professional tricks to preserve freshness, store loaves, and serve bakery treats like a pro.

How to Store Sourdough

Never refrigerate fresh bread! Keep cut-side down on a wooden cutting board or in a linen bread bag. To refresh day-old crust, mist lightly with water and bake at 200°C for 6 minutes.

Keeping Cakes Fresh

For buttercream cakes, refrigerate until 1 hour before serving. Bring to room temperature so the silky cocoa butter and dairy fats melt delicately on the palate.

Choosing Cake Tiers

Plan on 100g per guest for dessert-style cakes or 60g for coffee servings. A 2-tier 6"+8" cake effortlessly satisfies 30-35 happy party guests.

Resting Cookie Dough

Always chill your cookie dough for at least 24 hours before baking. This hydrates the flour starches and intensifies rich caramelized brown sugar notes.

Hydration Matters

A higher hydration dough (75-80%) yields an open, glossy crumb structure, while lower hydration doughs provide sturdy sandwich slicing.

Dessert & Wine Pairing

Pair 70% dark chocolate gateaux with bold Port or Cabernet, while lemon tarts and berry meringues shine beside crisp sparkling Prosecco.
