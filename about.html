Our Journey

Baking with Heart, Heritage, and Uncompromising Purity

Le Petit Four began with a simple copper whisk, a heritage sourdough starter named "Belle", and a dream to bring authentic French patisserie to our neighborhood.

Over the past 15 years, our kitchen has evolved into an artisanal sanctuary. We maintain time-honored slow-fermentation techniques that require 36 hours for a single loaf of bread, while introducing innovative flavor profiles to our celebration cakes and delicate pastries.

We never use pre-made mixes, artificial colorings, or hydrogenated oils. Every single butter layer is hand-rolled, every berry compote is simmered in small copper cauldrons, and every creation is crafted to elevate life's happiest moments.

Core Principles

Our Baking Philosophy

Four unwavering pillars guide every flour dusting, recipe development, and oven bake in our bakery.

01

Fresh Pure Ingredients

We source heritage grain varieties, single-origin Valrhona chocolate, fresh seasonal berries, and free-range dairy directly from sustainable farms.

02

Handmade Quality

No industrial shortcuts. Every croissant is laminated by hand, every tart crust is hand-pressed, and every cake tier is meticulously sculpted.

03

Traditional Taste

Rooted in centuries of classic European viennoiserie and bread-making tradition, honoring the authentic textures and aroma of wild fermentation.

04

Creative Modern Art

We blend timeless culinary fundamentals with contemporary architectural cake designs, delicate sugar flowers, and vibrant mirror glazes.

Milestones

Our Bakery Journey

A look back at our growth from a small artisan kitchen to a beloved city culinary landmark.

2011

Bakery Founded

Chef Jean-Pierre started baking in a humble 400 sq ft kitchen with a single stone oven and 3 signature sourdough recipes.

2014

First Flagship Patisserie

Opened our flagship café and bakery in downtown, expanding to full French viennoiseries and gourmet morning coffee.

2018

Signature Cake Studio

Launched our dedicated bespoke celebration and wedding cake studio, receiving the National Culinary Excellence Award.

2021

Online Ordering & Subscriptions

Introduced climate-controlled bakery delivery and weekly breakfast subscription boxes to serve households across the city.

2026

25,000+ Celebrations Milestone

Celebrating over 25,000 orders delivered, with a state-of-the-art kitchen facility and a community of passionate food lovers.
