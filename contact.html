Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about lead times, custom decorations, delivery coverage, and cancellations.

How far in advance should I order custom celebration cakes?

For standard birthday cakes, 48 hours notice is ideal. For multi-tier wedding cakes and intricate sculpted fondant designs, we recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance to reserve your date.

How does your temperature-controlled delivery service work?

Our dedicated delivery fleet features specialized cushioned cargo beds with climate stabilization between 16°C and 18°C. This ensures silk buttercream and delicate sugar florals arrive in pristine, unblemished condition.

Do you offer gluten-free, eggless, or vegan options?

Yes! We have dedicated eggless chocolate truffle cakes, gluten-friendly almond macarons, and oat milk brioches. Please specify your dietary restrictions when placing your order.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

Standard menu orders may be cancelled up to 24 hours prior for a full refund. Custom tiered cakes can be rescheduled or refunded up to 5 days prior to event dispatch.

Can I arrange corporate catering with invoice billing?

Absolutely. Corporate clients can access our dedicated Client Dashboard for Net-30 invoicing, recurring weekly breakfast orders, and branded pastry assortments.
